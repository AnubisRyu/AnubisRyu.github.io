Interests

A few interests that help explain what keeps me curious about technology.

Gaming

I enjoy gaming because it gives me a way to unwind, challenge myself, and connect with people who share the same interests. Games such as Destiny 2 and Marvel Rivals also involve communication, quick decision-making, teamwork, and problem-solving.

Technology

I follow technology across areas such as GPUs, CPUs, laptops, tablets, and handheld gaming devices. I enjoy learning how hardware and software work together and how new products improve performance and usability.

Personal Audio

I have also developed an interest in in-ear monitors and personal audio. Comparing tuning styles, frequency graphs, and driver technologies has become another way for me to explore how engineering and personal preference meet.
