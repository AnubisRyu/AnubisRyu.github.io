EU Artificial Intelligence Act

Policy Analysis Report

Project Overview

This report examines the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act and its impact on organizations, technology developers, and cybersecurity professionals. It demonstrates my ability to research technology policies, evaluate stakeholder impacts, identify cybersecurity concerns, and develop practical recommendations.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Technology policy research and analysis
  • Cybersecurity risk assessment
  • Stakeholder analysis
  • Critical thinking and professional recommendations
  • Understanding artificial intelligence security requirements

Key Findings

The analysis explores how the AI Act establishes requirements for different categories of AI systems. It also considers how organizations should evaluate AI-related risks, data access, security practices, and regulatory responsibilities.
