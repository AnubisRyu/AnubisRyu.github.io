About Me

My Story & Career Goals

My interests in technology and problem solving are shaping the direction I want to take after graduation.

Video 1: Tell Me About Yourself

I am a University of South Florida student with a strong interest in technology, programming, web development, and cybersecurity. Through my coursework and personal interests, I have developed experience with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and Java. I enjoy learning how technology works and finding practical ways to use it to solve problems. Outside of school, I am interested in gaming, computer hardware, and other areas of technology, which has helped keep me curious about how digital systems work. Cybersecurity is especially interesting to me because it combines technology, problem solving, and continuous learning. As I continue my education, I want to use my coursework and projects to build a strong foundation for my future career.

Video 2: What Am I Working Toward After Graduation?

After graduation, my goal is to earn a master's degree in cybersecurity and build a career in information security. I am working toward that goal by completing my undergraduate education, strengthening my technical skills, and gaining practical experience through projects and future technology-related opportunities. I plan to continue learning about networking, vulnerabilities, risk management, and other areas of cybersecurity, while also looking for internships, entry-level positions, certifications, or projects that can give me hands-on experience.
